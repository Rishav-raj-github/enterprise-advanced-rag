# Acme Corp Enterprise HR Policy Manual
**Document ID:** HR-2025-V4.2  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025  
**Applicability:** All Full-Time and Part-Time Employees globally  

---

## 1. Hybrid and Remote Work Policy
Acme Corp operates on a "flexible first" model. Employees are categorized into three working modes:
*   **Fully Remote:** Working 100% from a home office or approved co-working space. Eligible for a one-time remote setup stipend of $1,500.
*   **Hybrid:** Working 2-3 days per week from an Acme local hub. Eligible for a one-time remote setup stipend of $750.
*   **On-site:** Working 4-5 days per week from an Acme local hub. Eligible for commuter reimbursement up to $200/month.

### 1.1 Home Office Equipment Stipend
The remote work stipend covers ergonomic chairs, desks, monitors, and keyboards. All equipment purchased using this stipend is owned by the employee but must comply with security guidelines. For replacement or maintenance of company-issued laptops, contact IT Support directly.

## 2. Leave and Time-Off Policies
We believe in a healthy work-life balance and provide robust time-off benefits.

### 2.1 Annual Paid Time Off (PTO)
*   **Standard Accrual:** Employees accrue PTO at a rate of 1.67 days per month, totaling 20 business days per calendar year.
*   **Carryover Limit:** A maximum of 5 unused PTO days may carry over to the next calendar year. Any additional accrued PTO exceeding this limit on December 31 will be automatically forfeited unless prohibited by local jurisdiction.
*   **Acme Wellness Days:** The company provides 4 corporate wellness days annually (one per quarter), during which all offices are closed to allow mental rejuvenation.

### 2.2 Parental Leave
Acme Corp provides up to 16 weeks of 100% paid parental leave for birth, adoption, or foster placement of a child. This benefit is available to all eligible employees who have completed at least 6 months of continuous service. Parental leave must be taken within 12 months of the qualifying event.

## 3. Performance Review and Promotion Cycles
Performance cycles run semi-annually:
1.  **H1 Review Cycle:** Launches in June, focusing on accomplishments from January to May. Outcomes (adjustments, promotions) take effect on September 1.
2.  **H2 Review Cycle:** Launches in December, focusing on accomplishments from June to November. Outcomes take effect on March 1 of the following year.

Performance is rated on a 5-point scale:
*   `1`: Unsatisfactory Performance
*   `2`: Needs Development
*   `3`: Solid Performance (Meets Expectations)
*   `4`: Exceeds Expectations
*   `5`: Outstanding Performance

Promotions typically require at least two consecutive cycles with a rating of `4` or higher, accompanied by business justification and department head approval.
